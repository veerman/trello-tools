Board Lineage

Put boards that are versions of the same board into one line, oldest first. Side boards (drafts, test copies) stay in the line but out of its history. Every change is saved to lineage.json in the backup folder.

Not in a line

Drag a board onto a line, pick a line for it, or start a new line from it. Drop a board here to take it out of its line.
